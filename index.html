Why we only bake mornings

We started with one oven and a starter that's now old enough to have a name. The rule hasn't changed since: bake what sells that day, and stop when it's gone.

That means shorter hours than most bakeries, and an empty shelf by early afternoon — but every loaf that leaves the counter was mixed, proofed, and baked within the same twelve hours.
